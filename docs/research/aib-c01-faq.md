# AWS Certified AI Business Strategist FAQ research

Verified against AWS sources on November 19, 2026.

## What is AIB-C01, and who is it for?

AIB-C01 is the exam code for AWS Certified AI Business Strategist. The Business certification assesses how professionals evaluate AI investments, build business cases, establish responsible governance, and scale AI adoption. AWS lists product and program managers, consultants, business analysts, sales and marketing professionals, and business leaders among its intended candidates. [AWS certification overview](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/).

AWS recommends about six months of experience working with or alongside AI initiatives, basic AI literacy, and awareness of AWS AI offerings at a business level. Coding, hands-on AWS implementation experience, and prior AWS certifications are optional. [AWS certification overview](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/), [AIB-C01 exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-business-strategist-01/ai-business-strategist-01.html#target-candidate-description).

## How many questions are on the exam, and how long does it take?

AWS lists 85 multiple-choice and multiple-response questions and 170 minutes for the beta. The beta costs USD 50 and is available in English and Japanese at a Pearson testing center or online with a proctor. AWS lists USD 100 as the standard price. [AWS certification overview](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/).

The exam guide separately lists 130 minutes in its general exam content section. Use the certification page's explicitly labeled duration for beta information. AWS's general policy says beta exams have more total and scored questions than their standard versions. [AIB-C01 exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-business-strategist-01/ai-business-strategist-01.html#exam-content), [AWS beta exam policy](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/).

## How do questions and scoring work?

Multiple-choice questions have one correct option out of four. Multiple-response questions have at least two correct options out of five or more, and AWS requires all correct selections for credit. A blank answer counts as incorrect, and wrong guesses carry no extra penalty. The minimum passing scaled score is 700 on a 100–1,000 scale. The pass decision applies to the exam overall. [AIB-C01 exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-business-strategist-01/ai-business-strategist-01.html#exam-content).

## How does AIB-C01 differ from AIF-C01?

AWS Certified AI Business Strategist emphasizes investment decisions, business cases, governance, and adoption. AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) covers foundational AI, machine learning, generative AI, and AWS AI services. [AWS certification overview](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/), [AIF-C01 exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-practitioner-01/ai-practitioner-01.html).
